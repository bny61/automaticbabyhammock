# Idea index

Ideas raised in [brainstorming sessions](../sessions/). **None of these is authoritative** — an
idea graduates by becoming an [ADR](../../docs/04-decisions/index.md), a
[trade study](../../docs/05-trade-studies/index.md), a
[requirement](../../docs/01-requirements/index.md) or a
[hazard](../../docs/03-safety/hazard-analysis.md).

Allocate an ID with `python tools/new_idea.py "Title"`.

## Status

`raw` just captured · `promising` worth developing · `parked` good, wrong time ·
`rejected` tried and failed — **kept deliberately**

## Why rejected ideas stay here

An idea that was considered and rejected is information. Delete it and it gets re-proposed in six
months and re-evaluated from scratch — or worse, adopted by someone who does not know why it
failed the first time. Rejected entries keep their file and their reasoning.
